# Easy Lesson — 30-Day Content Calendar

Site: Easy Lesson — https://www.easylesson.example
Tool: Lesson Solver AI (homepage, /#tool) — snap a photo of a textbook page → step-by-step exercise solutions, bullet notes, audio pronunciation, interactive quizzes. Users enter their own free Google AI Studio API key inside the tool.
Audience: Worldwide / global. Site language: English.

## Cadence

One post per day for 30 days. Days 1–10 publish the 10 starter posts. Days 11–30 publish the 20 new topics from the keyword plan, in order. Each post targets one focus keyword and answers one clear reader question. Link each post to the homepage tool (/#tool) and to 2–3 related posts where genuinely helpful.

**Important:** The 10 starter posts are drafts to review/edit before publishing. Review each draft for accuracy, clarity, and originality, fix anything that reads as thin or generic, and only then publish on its scheduled date. Keep publishing on schedule while later posts are being drafted — do not wait until all 30 are finished to start publishing.
