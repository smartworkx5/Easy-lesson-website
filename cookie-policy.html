Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies Easy Lesson uses, why, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. Some are set by this site ("first-party" cookies); others may be set by third-party services this site uses. This site also uses browser local storage for a couple of preferences — it works like a cookie but stays on your device.

What we use today

  • Preference storage (first-party, local storage): el_theme remembers your dark/light mode choice; el_cookie_consent remembers your Accept/Decline choice from the cookie banner. These are set on your device only and are not sent to us.
  • Tool storage (first-party, local storage, set by the tool): the embedded AI lesson solver stores your API key in your browser so you do not have to re-enter it. Easy Lesson itself never sees your key.
  • Fonts: this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may set its own cookies or log requests per Google's policies.

What may be added later

  • Analytics cookies (only if the owner enables analytics): a service such as Google Analytics would set cookies to count visits and measure which pages are useful. These are only set after the owner configures analytics — none is active by default.
  • Advertising cookies (only if the owner enables ads after AdSense approval): Google AdSense may set cookies to serve and measure ads. Advertising cookies are only set after the owner enables ads, and the cookie banner is designed to be consent-aware: visitors in regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK) should be asked before non-essential cookies are set. The site owner must connect a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) before serving personalised ads in those regions — see the README and adsense-guide.md.

Managing your choices

  • Cookie banner: use Accept to allow cookies, or Decline to refuse non-essential cookies. Your choice is remembered on your device.
  • Change your mind: clear your browser's cookies and site data for this site, then reload — the banner will appear again.
  • Browser controls: every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking essential storage may reset your theme preference and your API key inside the tool.

Changes to this policy

If we start using new cookies (for example, by enabling analytics or advertising), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
