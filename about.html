About Easy Lesson

We built Easy Lesson around a simple belief: every learner deserves a patient tutor for the exact question they are stuck on — available the moment they sit down to study.

What Easy Lesson Is

Easy Lesson is a free AI lesson solver for learners worldwide. You snap a photo of a textbook page or exercise sheet, and the tool walks you through the lesson: step-by-step exercise solutions, short bullet notes, audio pronunciation, and a quick quiz to test yourself. There is no signup, nothing to install, and no question bank to search through — it responds to the actual page in front of you.

The tool works with textbook pages in English, Urdu and Sindhi, and can explain solutions in the language you choose. The website itself is in English.

Why We Built It

Getting stuck on homework is a lonely experience. The teacher is not there, the textbook shows the question but not the method, and the answer key — if one exists — shows the result but not the reasoning. We wanted a tool that closes that gap: one that shows the working, explains each step, and helps the learner solve the next question on their own.

Easy Lesson is designed as a learning aid, not an answer machine. That is why every solution shows its steps, why our blog talks honestly about checking AI answers against your textbook, and why we encourage learners to try a question first and use the tool to compare and understand.

Who Uses Easy Lesson

  • Students — to check homework, understand the steps behind an answer, revise a chapter, and quiz themselves before a test.
  • Teachers — to prepare worked examples, build practice sets from a lesson, and find a second explanation for a tricky exercise.
  • Parents — to understand a lesson quickly so they can help with homework.
  • Tutors — to turn a textbook page into worked solutions, notes and a quiz for the next session.

How the Tool Works

The tool on our homepage connects to an AI service using your own free Google AI Studio API key, which you paste inside the tool. Your key stays in your own browser, and Easy Lesson itself does not store your photos. The AI reads your textbook photo, extracts the exercises, and returns step-by-step solutions, bullet notes, audio pronunciation and a quiz. Because no AI tool is always correct, we always recommend verifying important answers against your textbook or teacher — see our Disclaimer.

Our Principles

  • Learning first. Show the method, not just the answer.
  • Honest about limits. AI can make mistakes; we say so plainly and encourage checking.
  • Private by default. No account required, and we do not store your photos.
  • Free to use. The tool is free; you only need your own free API key.
