Easy Lesson is a study aid, not a substitute for teaching

Everything on Easy Lesson — including the AI lesson solver tool and the blog articles — is provided to help you learn. It is not a substitute for your textbook, your teacher, or your own study. Always verify AI-generated solutions against your textbook or teacher before relying on them, especially for assessed work.

AI-generated content can be wrong

AI tools can misread a photo, misunderstand a question, or produce a confident-sounding answer that contains a mistake. That is why Easy Lesson always shows the working behind each solution: so you can check every step. If a step looks wrong, re-take the photo more clearly, try again, or ask your teacher.
