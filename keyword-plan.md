# Easy Lesson — Keyword Plan

Site: Easy Lesson — https://www.easylesson.example
Tool: Lesson Solver AI — snap a photo of a textbook page → step-by-step exercise solutions, bullet notes, audio pronunciation, interactive quizzes. The tool lives on the homepage at /#tool. Users enter their own free Google AI Studio API key inside the tool.
Audience: Worldwide / global. Site language: English.
Multilingual note: The tool supports textbook photos in English, Urdu, and Sindhi as a multilingual feature.

This plan defines the keywords the site targets and the blog topics built from them. All content must be original. No fake reviews, statistics, or testimonials. No keyword stuffing — use keywords naturally where they fit the reader's question.

---

## Primary Keyword

### AI lesson solver

- **Intent:** Mixed informational + commercial — people looking for a tool that solves lesson and textbook questions from a photo.
- **Why it fits:** It names exactly what Easy Lesson does — an AI tool that works lesson by lesson, from a textbook photo to a worked solution — and it is broad enough to cover exercises, notes, and quizzes without limiting the site to one subject or one country.
