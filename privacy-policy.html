Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Easy Lesson is designed to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what data this website collects, what it does not collect, and how the tool on the homepage handles your information.

The short version

  • Easy Lesson does not require an account.
  • Easy Lesson does not store your textbook photos.
  • Your Google AI Studio API key is stored in your own browser by the tool — Easy Lesson never sees it.
  • We do not sell your data.

What the tool does with your photos and API key

The AI lesson solver on the homepage runs in your browser and connects to an AI service using your own free Google AI Studio API key, which you paste inside the tool. The key is kept in your browser's local storage by the tool itself. Photos you upload are sent from your browser to the AI service that your key connects to, so that the service can read the page and generate solutions. Easy Lesson's own servers do not receive, store, or process your photos or your key.

Because your photos and key go to a third-party AI service, that service's own privacy terms also apply. You can clear your key and your browser storage at any time through your browser settings.

What this website collects

  • Contact form: if you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you type, so we can reply. The form is connected to a form service configured by the site owner (see README). We use your message only to respond to you.
  • Preferences: your dark/light mode choice and your cookie-consent choice are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Analytics (only if enabled): if the site owner adds Google Analytics or a similar service in the future, that service may collect anonymised usage data such as pages visited and device type. No analytics service is active on this site until the owner configures it — the placeholder is documented in the README.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies and local-storage entries, mainly to remember your preferences and your cookie-consent choice. If advertising is enabled in the future (see the Cookie Policy), the advertising provider may set its own cookies. You can accept or decline cookies using the banner on the site, and you can change your mind by clearing your browser storage.

Children's privacy

Easy Lesson is a general-audience study site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information through the contact form, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services

This site may embed or link to third-party services: the AI service connected by your own API key (Google AI Studio), fonts served by Google Fonts, and — only if the owner enables them — analytics and advertising services. Each provider has its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to read them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version on this page. Significant changes will be noted clearly on the homepage.
